# Glossary

## Dual-Loop
A practice mode where two loops with different subdivisions alternate at the same BPM. Loop 1 uses a slower subdivision (e.g., 8th-note triplets = 3 notes/beat), Loop 2 uses a faster one (e.g., sextuplets = 6 notes/beat). The subdivision ratio (e.g., 1:2) is the speed multiplier the player experiences.
_Avoid_:"tempo cycling" (that implies different BPMs per loop — this app uses the same BPM, only subdivisions differ)

## Rest Beats
A configurable number of beats between Loop 1 and Loop 2 where the metronome clicks the beat but no subdivision clicks. Gives the player time to switch licks or picking style. Default: 1 beat. Setting to 0 produces an immediate switch.

## Subdivision Ratio
The ratio of notes-per-second between two subdivisions at the same BPM. E.g., 8th-note triplets (3/beat) to sextuplets (6/beat) = 1:2 ratio. This is the speed-building mechanism: the player experiences double the speed while the beat stays constant.

## Sextuplet
Six notes per beat. Synonymous with 16th-note triplets. The app uses "sextuplet" as the label since that is the term guitar players commonly use.
_Avoid_:"16th-note triplet" (same thing, but guitar players say "sextuplet")

## Progressive Tempo
A practice mode where BPM automatically increases after a configurable number of bars, following a curve (linear, logarithmic, stepback, or pyramid). Covers tempo ladders like 60→70→75→80→85.
